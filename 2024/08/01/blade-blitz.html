Blade Blitz

Blade Blitz is a competitive local-multiplayer sword fighting game inspired by the tension and simplicity of Nidhogg, reimagined as a fast-paced 3v3 spectator sport. Designed and developed solo, the project explores how bold, sports-inspired visual systems can elevate clarity, energy, and competitive drama in a chaotic multiplayer environment.

I am the solo designer and developer of Blade Blitz, responsible for the game’s visual identity, UI/UX design, branding, and implementation. My role spans concept exploration, interface design, motion and feedback systems, style guide creation, and real-world playtesting through public showcases. I also documented the process through social media to gather feedback and build an audience throughout development.
