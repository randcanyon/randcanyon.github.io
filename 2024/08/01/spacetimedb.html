SpacetimeDB

Created a logo animation visualizing the gravitational distortion of light around a black hole, bringing the SpacetimeDB identity to life through motion.

I designed and animated the logo reveal for SpacetimeDB, a distributed database technology project by Clockwork Labs. Using Blender and After Effects, I created a visual narrative inspired by gravitational lensing, light bending around a black hole, culminating in the reveal of the logo. The animation established a cinematic yet scientific tone that reflected the brand’s focus on scale, depth, and innovation.

Role

  • Conceptualized and storyboarded the motion identity inspired by black hole physics and gravitational light distortion.
  • Modeled and animated the sequence in Blender, using procedural light effects to simulate lensing and motion depth.
  • Composited and polished the final render in After Effects, ensuring smooth transitions and precise logo integration.
