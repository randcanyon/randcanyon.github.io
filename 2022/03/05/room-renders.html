Room Renders

A personal 3D art exercise where I modeled and rendered my apartment during lockdown.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I challenged myself to learn Blender by modeling and rendering different sections of my apartment. Working in an orthographic perspective, I treated each room as both a design study and a storytelling canvas.

Context

During the COVID-19 lockdown, I used the time to learn Blender from scratch. Rather than following tutorials, I set myself a concrete project: model and render the spaces I was living in. The apartment became the subject. Each room got its own render, treated as a small design problem — how to convey space, light, and personality in a flat orthographic view.
