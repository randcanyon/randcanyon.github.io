You're Not Alone

A fast-paced game developed in under a week for the Brackeys Game Jam 2022.2.

You're Not Alone is a short game I designed and developed for the Brackeys Game Jam 2022.2. Built in under a week around the theme "You're Not Alone," the project placed 58th out of 1,075 entries and served as an exercise in rapid prototyping and cohesive visual design under tight creative constraints.

Context

The Brackeys Game Jam gives participants one week and a theme. The constraint is the point; there is no time to over-engineer, so every decision has to be immediate and committed. For 2022.2, the theme was You're Not Alone. The entire scope of concept, design, development, and polish had to fit inside that window.
