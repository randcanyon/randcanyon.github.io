You're Not Alone

You're Not Alone is a short game I designed and developed for the Brackeys Game Jam 2022.2. Built in under a week around the theme “You’re Not Alone,” the project placed 58th out of 1,075 entries and served as an exercise in rapid prototyping and cohesive visual design under tight creative constraints.
