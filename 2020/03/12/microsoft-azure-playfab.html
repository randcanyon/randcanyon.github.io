Microsoft Azure Playfab Awards @ Microsoft

A modular physical award system celebrating PlayFab customer growth, designed to transform digital milestones into sculptural, collectible recognition pieces.

Azure PlayFab wanted a way to celebrate studios reaching major player-growth milestones on their platform. I designed a series of physical trophies—marking 100K, 1M, and 10M players—that could stand alone or interlock into a unified sculptural form. My role spanned concept development, 3D modeling, and high-fidelity visualization, exploring how material, geometry, and modularity could bring digital success to life in a tangible and memorable way. These prototypes provided a clear direction for fabrication and established a unique PlayFab recognition experience.

Problem

Game developers often used Microsoft tools: Azure PlayFab, Visual Studio, Xbox Live, Direct, etc. But the ecosystem was fragmented. Each product had its own look and story, resulting in:

Growth
Represented customer loyalty and progress in a visually striking, meaningful way.
Modular System
Worked as individual awards, but also connected into a larger story when collected.
Competing Brands
Marketing materials that felt disjointed and lacked a shared brand language.
Feasible Construction
Was clear enough for fabrication partners to understand scale, materials, and assembly.

Outcomes & Impact

The project resulted in a modular award system that visually stacks and evolves as a studio grows from 100K to 10M players. I delivered a complete visual direction through 3D models, turntable renders, and material explorations, helping stakeholders align on form, finish, and packaging. We produced physical prototypes and evaluated them with two fabrication vendors. Although the program ultimately did not move forward for customer distribution, the work established a clear foundation for how PlayFab could celebrate developer milestones and demonstrated the potential of physical recognition within the Azure ecosystem.
