Microsoft Azure Playfab Awards @ Microsoft

A modular award system translating PlayFab growth milestones into collectible physical trophies.

Designed a trophy series for 100K, 1M, and 10M player milestones, using modular forms and 3D visualization to guide stakeholder alignment and fabrication.

Challenge

PlayFab needed milestone awards that felt premium, scalable, and feasible to manufacture.

Growth
Needed to represent customer progress in a visually meaningful way.
Modular System
Each award had to stand alone while also connecting into a larger story.
Feasible Construction
The concept had to be clear enough for fabrication partners to price and build.

Outcomes & Impact

The concept established a clear award direction, produced prototypes, and gave stakeholders a concrete fabrication path even though the program did not ship.

Modular Trophy Series
Created three awards that work individually and combine into a larger growth story.
Fabrication-Ready Direction
Delivered renders and physical prototypes that clarified material, finish, and assembly.
Clear Milestone Recognition
Gave PlayFab a more memorable way to celebrate customer growth than a generic plaque.
