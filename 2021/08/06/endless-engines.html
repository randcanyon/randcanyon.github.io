Endless Engines

A 3D animation created for the Endless Engines 3D art challenge.

I created a submission for the Endless Engines community challenge run by Clint Jones, where artists built animated 3D vehicle scenes from a shared template. Using a sci-fi theme, I designed a futuristic freeway chase featuring a drone as the centerpiece. This project was an opportunity to strengthen my skills in modeling, texturing, and animation within a focused creative brief, culminating in a render showcased in the official community montage.

Context

Endless Engines was a community 3D art challenge where all participants started from the same vehicle template. The brief was open: pick a theme, build a world around it, and animate the result. I went sci-fi, designing a drone chasing through a futuristic freeway. The shared starting point makes the challenge useful as a skill exercise — the creative decisions are fully exposed with no room to hide behind the model.
